Visual Intelligence & Inventory Integrity Analytics

A synthetic-data portfolio project examining visual-audit quality outcomes and their association with subsequent inventory discrepancies and pick incidents.

View GitHub Repository

Business Problem

Visual-audit defects may be associated with downstream inventory discrepancies and pick incidents. This project establishes consistent KPI definitions and analyzes the relationship between independent review outcomes and subsequent operational events.

Tools and Methods

PostgreSQL · Power BI · DAX · Excel · Relational Data Modeling · KPI Validation · Data Quality Checks · Business Recommendations

Analysis Delivered

  • Reviewed-sample quality metrics and data validation.
  • Seven-day discrepancy and pick-incident analysis.
  • Defect-category, team, tenure, shift, and handling-time comparisons.
  • Power BI reporting and operational recommendations.

Recommendations

  • Investigate defect categories with elevated observed discrepancy rates.
  • Connect quality-review findings to downstream event records.
  • Assess team and training patterns alongside case mix and sample size.
  • Test process improvements before scaling them.

Limitations

All data are synthetic. The observed differences are associations, not evidence that a review defect caused a subsequent event. Results are illustrative and are not production performance estimates.
